<h2>About the Working Groups</h2>
<p>The Pelagios Network focuses on core aspects of linked data via Working Groups.</p>
<p>Working Groups meet every two months to hear from partners, share and develop good practice, and offer advice. Working Groups also develop collaboration among partners (e.g. funding bids, sharing resources),  creates tool documentation (e.g. tutorials) and share pedagogical ideas. we also welcome guest speakers from outside academia, such as from journalism or commerce.</p>
<p>Each Working Group is run by two Coordinators elected from the Partners of that Activity. The Coordinators from all the Working Groups meet once a month.</p>
<p>If you would like to join a particular group, please contact the Coordinators via the relevant address below.</p>
